FAQ

This is a game right?

No, it's an experience! You go around your neighborhood, you find stuff that you didn't notice before, and you share it with the rest of the world. Doesn't that sound fun!


Okay, what do I need?

All you need is your phone (or a laptop and a camera). Once you have that, go outside, snap a picture, go back inside and finsh up your junk posting.


Who can participate?

We're trying to keep the game only for teenagers ages 13-18 right now, but maybe some time in the future you can join in, too!


So, what will I be doing?

Well, it's up to you. Every 30 minutes, a new "piece of junk" will be generated. You go and find it, and post a picture of it to the Junkyard. After that, the rest of Hack Club has the opportunity to vote on your "junk". If more people think you actually followed through with the post, you get extra junk!


Are there prizes?

Uh... yeah... The prize is you getting to know other people better. You get to see what their life is like. (Man, that was good!)
